THE PUBLIC LOG

Every trade is public.

Losses are posted the same way as wins. There are four places the record is kept, and each can be checked against the others.

Each trade
Posted to the Ascent Discord in {{rule.posting}} — entry, strike, stop and exit.
Running record
The Ascent dashboard shows account equity and every closed trade.
Weekly
One summary with equity, the change from the {{start.usd}} start, and the status of each rule.
Monthly
A broker statement confirming the balance.
