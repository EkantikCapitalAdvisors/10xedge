WHAT TEN TIMES TAKES

Three doublings and a third.

$3,000 to $6,000 to $12,000 to $24,000 is three doublings. The last stretch to $30,000 is a third of one more. Here is what each kind of year would deliver.

Four scenarios for the year, by doublings earned. All hypothetical.
The yearDoublings earnedWhat it means
Cooperativeevery buffer is earned, none is given back up to 4 (16×) Clears ten times. The ceiling is held at four doublings until a live record exists.
Mixedsome buffers earned; sit-outs and reverts 1–3 (2–8×) Falls short of ten times. The protocol itself calls this the realistic envelope.
Hostilestand-downs dominate 0–0.5 (about 1–1.5×) Roughly flat, or down. Size never leaves the first step.
Falsifiedthe Expression Gate fires — The experiment stops and says so. Research only until the edge is re-proven.

Hypothetical. These ranges are design arithmetic from the published protocol — not results, and not a forecast. Hypothetical performance has inherent limitations: it does not reflect actual trading and cannot account for real market conditions. Three of the four rows end short of the target.

Ten times in a year is the objective. It is not a forecast, and nothing on this page is a promise that it happens.
