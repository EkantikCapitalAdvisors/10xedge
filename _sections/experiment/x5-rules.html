THE PROTOCOL

Size is earned, never chosen.

The account trades one size until it has won {{protocol.buffer}} uncorrelated trades’ worth of premium. Then size doubles. Give that cushion back and size steps down again — on the next entry, without discussion.

THE EARNED-DOUBLING LADDER

At a {{protocol.unit}} premium unit the first cushion is {{protocol.firstBuffer}}, and each one after it is twice the last. The four of them add up to {{protocol.ladderSum}} — the whole distance from $3,000 to $30,000. That is arithmetic, not a projection.

  • Uncorrelated is a bright-line test of entry session, directional view and class of underlying — not a judgment call. Two SPX trades in one session count once.
  • No early step. The ladder moves when the balance reaches the milestone, or it does not move. Confidence is not an input.
  • After a revert the next step needs a fresh cushion at the smaller size. The old one does not carry over.
