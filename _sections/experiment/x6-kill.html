HOW IT STOPS

Eight conditions. All of them armed.

An edge can fade, or its operator can drift. Each failure has its own alarm, written down before the first trade, and none of them asks for an opinion.

EXPRESSION GATE

Is the edge still positive?

If expectancy over the last 100 trades falls to $0 per trade or below, new trading stops. The gate reads provisionally from 80 trades and binds at 100.

FIDELITY GATE

Are the rules still being followed?

  • Every trade is attributed.
  • Every rule change is countersigned and disclosed before it runs.
  • Every step up and every step down is logged.
  1. 01Expectancy over the last 100 trades at or below $0Stop. Research only.
  2. 02One realised loss in a dayStop for the day.
  3. 03The weekly loss cap is reachedStand down for the week.
  4. 04Two losing weeks in a rowStand down for the month.
  5. 05A cushion is given backSize reverts one step.
  6. 06Two reverts within 14 daysPause for witness review.
  7. 07A trade without attributionFidelity breach.
  8. 08A fidelity breach still openGate readings are suspended until it is fixed.

INDEPENDENT WITNESS

Who can overrule the operator?

The call on a breach is not the operator’s to make, and a rule change is not the operator’s to approve. Every stand-down, revert and gate event is posted in Discord within 24 hours. For this protocol the witness’s countersignature is still pending — it is stated here because a rulebook that hides its own gaps is not one.

Failure is public by design. If the edge is not there, this page is where you will see it first.
