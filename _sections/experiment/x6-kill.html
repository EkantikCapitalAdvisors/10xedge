KILL CRITERIA

Three things end it early.

The experiment stops if any one of these happens. Each is published in advance, and none of them asks for an opinion.

  1. 1

    Floor

    Account equity closes at or below {{rule.floor}}. Trading stops. A final report follows within {{rule.floorDays}} days.

  2. 2

    Rule breach

    Any trade outside the rules above. The experiment ends and is reported as a breach, not a result.

  3. 3

    Edge check

    Expectancy over the last {{rule.edge}} trades turns negative. Trading pauses until the witness reviews it.

If none of these happens, the experiment ends on {{end.date}}.

INDEPENDENT WITNESS

Who checks the operator?

None of the three needs anyone’s permission to fire. If the design does not work, this page is where you will see it.
