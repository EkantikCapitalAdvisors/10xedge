About the track record. Figures reflect 149 live trades over approximately six months (Feb–Aug 2026) in a single market regime, called in real time in our Discord channel and net of all costs. This is a limited, single-regime record. Past performance is not indicative of future results; the edge may weaken or disappear.

Basis of the record, and how it was narrowed. The record counts only trades called in our Discord channel before the outcome was known, so the attribution above is true of every trade in it. An earlier, broader version of this record also included trades recorded from broker statements rather than from the Discord log. Those trades performed less well than the Discord-called ones, so narrowing the record to the Discord-called basis raised most of the figures on this page. We state the direction of that change rather than leave a reader to find it: the win rate, profit factor and per-trade tilt are higher on this basis than on the broader one, and the sample is smaller. The Discord log is timestamped but is not an audited record, and the trades are not independently broker-reconciled.

Risk-adjusted figures. The Sharpe estimate (~1.4–1.7) is trade-derived and conditional on the edge persisting — not the naive pre-haircut figure. No specific rate of return is promised or implied. Renaissance Medallion and aviation-safety (Airbus) figures are reported or estimated third-party context, not audited, and are used only as scale and analogy.

Ruin and capacity figures. Ruin and drawdown figures are Monte-Carlo simulations — 200,000 five-year paths, with win/loss outcomes drawn from the Discord-logged record, losses capped near 1R, and trades treated as independent — conditional on the edge being real out-of-sample. Live results can exceed the model. “Engineered toward zero” describes a design and risk-management philosophy, not an assurance that ruin will be avoided. Capacity figures ($3M at launch, stretching toward approximately $5M) are the strategy’s own estimate and may be revised down as the edge is measured live.

Risk of loss. Trading futures involves a substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for every investor. You can lose money, and losses can exceed the amount initially committed. Only risk capital should be used.
