Three doublings and a third.

$3,000 to $6,000 to $12,000 to $24,000 is three doublings. The last stretch to $30,000 is a third of one more. Here is what each kind of year would deliver.

WHY OPTIONS

A known loss. An open-ended gain.

A long option costs its premium and can lose no more than that. If price travels through the strike before expiry, the payoff steepens. That shape is why the experiment runs here — and why it can fail quickly.

WHY IT CAN WORK

The risk is known to the dollar at entry: the premium. The reward depends on how far price moves before expiry. One winner can pay for several losers — if the winners come.

WHY IT FAILS

Short-dated options are brittle. In a choppy market the premium decays faster than price moves, and losses arrive in clusters. The rules react to realised losses; they do not try to predict the market.

THE PROTOCOL

Size is earned, never chosen.

The account trades one size until it has won six uncorrelated trades’ worth of premium. Then size doubles. Give that cushion back and size steps down again — on the next entry, without discussion.

THE EARNED-DOUBLING LADDER

  1. 1×

    Earn $1,800

    next step at $4,800

  2. 2×

    Earn $3,600

    next step at $8,400

  3. 4×

    Earn $7,200

    next step at $15,600

  4. 8×

    Earn $14,400

    reaches $30,000

At a $300 premium unit the first cushion is $1,800, and each one after it is twice the last. The four of them add up to $27,000 — the whole distance from $3,000 to $30,000. That is arithmetic, not a projection.

  • Uncorrelated is a bright-line test of entry session, directional view and class of underlying — not a judgment call. Two SPX trades in one session count once.
  • No early step. The ladder moves when the balance reaches the milestone, or it does not move. Confidence is not an input.
  • After a revert the next step needs a fresh cushion at the smaller size. The old one does not carry over.

HOW IT STOPS

Eight conditions. All of them armed.

An edge can fade, or its operator can drift. Each failure has its own alarm, written down before the first trade, and none of them asks for an opinion.

EXPRESSION GATE

Is the edge still positive?

If expectancy over the last 100 trades falls to $0 per trade or below, new trading stops. The gate reads provisionally from 80 trades and binds at 100.

FIDELITY GATE

Are the rules still being followed?

  • Every trade is attributed.
  • Every rule change is countersigned and disclosed before it runs.
  • Every step up and every step down is logged.
  1. 01Expectancy over the last 100 trades at or below $0Stop. Research only.
  2. 02One realised loss in a dayStop for the day.
  3. 03The weekly loss cap is reachedStand down for the week.
  4. 04Two losing weeks in a rowStand down for the month.
  5. 05A cushion is given backSize reverts one step.
  6. 06Two reverts within 14 daysPause for witness review.
  7. 07A trade without attributionFidelity breach.
  8. 08A fidelity breach still openGate readings are suspended until it is fixed.

INDEPENDENT WITNESS

Who can overrule the operator?

The call on a breach is not the operator’s to make, and a rule change is not the operator’s to approve. Every stand-down, revert and gate event is posted in Discord within 24 hours. For this protocol the witness’s countersignature is still pending — it is stated here because a rulebook that hides its own gaps is not one.

Failure is public by design. If the edge is not there, this page is where you will see it first.
