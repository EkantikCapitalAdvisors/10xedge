  • 1LOAD LIMIT

    How much stress before it breaks?

    A wing is rated far beyond any storm — you never fly near the limit. Here: one capped chip (R) per trade, deliberate over-capitalization, and a hard 20% ceiling on total risk at any moment.

  • 2THE BACKUPS

    Will anything catch a failure?

    Independent backups that don’t share a failure mode. One gate watches the edge for decay; another watches the operator for drift. No single point of failure — including us.

  • 3THE SAFE DEFAULT

    What happens automatically when something fails?

    A tripped gate defaults to smaller size or a full stand-down — by rule, not willpower. It never depends on anyone being heroic in the moment.

  • 4THE LIVING TEST

    The question the sky never asks.

    A wing’s physics never changes — certify it once, done. A market adapts. So the system keeps testing itself, every day, with kill-criteria that never retire. The first three keep the account alive — this one keeps it right.
